Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Janell's Run ("the App") is a personal running tracker for iOS. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

HealthKit Data

The App reads running workout data from Apple HealthKit (distance, duration, and date) to display your race history and personal records. This data is used solely within the App to provide its core functionality. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Sign in with Apple

The App uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. Your Apple ID credentials are handled entirely by Apple. The App stores only the user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name. These are stored securely in the Keychain on your device and are not sent anywhere by the App.

iCloud Sync

Your run data is synced across your devices using Apple CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Only you can access your synced data.

Feedback Feature

If you submit a bug report or feature request through the App, the following information is sent to our server: your feedback text, device model, iOS version, and app version. This information is used solely to create an issue in the App's public GitHub repository, so anything you write in your feedback can be read by anyone. Your name is not sent.

Athlinks Import

If you choose to import race results from Athlinks, the App sends the Athlinks athlete ID you enter to athlinks.com to fetch that profile's results. The athlete ID is saved on your device so you can sync again later, and is removed if you delete your account. Athlinks' handling of that request is governed by its own privacy policy. The App does not send your run data to Athlinks.

Apart from what is described in this policy, the App does not collect or transmit personal data.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not include any analytics frameworks, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking. No data is collected for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

All run data is stored on your device using SwiftData and synced to your iCloud account via CloudKit. The App does not operate its own servers for storing user data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the in-app feedback feature or by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
